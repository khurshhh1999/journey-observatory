# Modeled opportunity

The pro forma prices the high-friction group found by the ranking.

Three lines stay separate. Gross recovered bookings are the bookings the recovery assumption would bring back. Margin is that amount times the margin rate. Avoidable support cost is contacts times the share assumed avoidable, times handling time, times cost. The modeled total is margin plus avoidable support cost. Gross bookings are not added in, so the same stay is not counted as both revenue and margin.

The figure is annualized from the extract window. It is a modeled estimate. It is not realized revenue, recovered cash, or profit.

Observed loss is the extract itself, with no recovery haircut: margin on the dropped journeys plus the full support cost of the contacts. Recoverable opportunity is the annualized base case. Forecast benefit follows the scenario being edited. Post-launch evidence is empty until a fix has been measured. Those four are not the same number wearing four labels.

One-way sensitivity moves a single assumption and leaves the others on the base. The low, base, and high table is the downside, base, and upside scenario.

The contact reduction is simulated on a fixed workload. The workload does not change when the sliders move. Only the share assumed avoidable changes.

Assumptions live in the model config. Change them there and run the pipeline again. The dashboard sliders apply the same arithmetic on top of the exported inputs.
